How to use this guide

Compare the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and Germany for courses, costs, post-study work, and possible pathways.

  • Start with official requirements and provider information.
  • Avoid any advice that promises guaranteed admission, visa approval, jobs, scholarships, or migration.
  • Document your assumptions and review them when rules change.
