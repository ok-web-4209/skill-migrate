Student visa and post-study work visa guides

Understand common student visa requirements, post-study work options, and possible study-to-work pathways for major destinations. Visa outcomes depend on eligibility and current rules.

Short introduction

A student visa lets eligible students study in a specific country under specific conditions. Some destinations also offer post-study work options that may help graduates gain experience after completing an eligible qualification.

Rules can change quickly. Always verify official sources, read the exact eligibility requirements for your course and institution, and consult licensed professionals for personal visa advice.

Student visa basics

Most student visa systems review whether you have a genuine study plan, admission to a recognised institution, enough funds, appropriate documents, health or insurance cover where required, and a clear understanding of your visa conditions.

Admission proof

Offer letters, enrolment confirmations, CAS, I-20, or similar documents may be required depending on the country.

Financial evidence

Many countries require proof that tuition and living costs can be covered. Exact evidence rules should be checked officially.

Visa conditions

Work hours, course changes, attendance, health insurance, address updates, and dependants can all be controlled by visa conditions.

Post-study work options overview

Post-study work options may allow eligible graduates to gain local experience after study. They are not automatic and may depend on course level, provider status, qualification length, occupation, location, timing, and application rules.

Safety note: Post-study work is a possible pathway only. It does not guarantee a job, employer sponsorship, permanent residence, or long-term migration. Verify official government sources before making decisions.

Country visa cards

USA F-1 Visa and OPT

The F-1 route is commonly used for academic study. Eligible students may use OPT, and some STEM graduates may qualify for extended practical training, subject to current rules. Read the USA guide.

UK Student Visa and Graduate Route

The UK Student visa supports eligible study at licensed sponsors. The Graduate Route may allow eligible graduates to work after study, subject to current UK rules. Read the UK guide.

Canada Study Permit and PGWP

A Canadian study permit is linked to eligible study. PGWP eligibility depends on institution, program, length, level, and current policy settings. Read the Canada guide.

Australia Student Visa and Graduate Visa

Australia's student visa has study, financial, health, and genuine student checks. Graduate visa options depend on eligibility and current rules. Read the Australia guide.

New Zealand Student Visa and Post-Study Work

Eligible students may study in New Zealand and later apply for post-study work if the qualification and circumstances meet current requirements. Read the New Zealand guide.

Germany Student Visa and Post-Study Options

Germany may offer post-study job search or skilled work options for eligible graduates. Language ability and recognised qualifications can matter. Read the Germany guide.

Common mistakes

  • Applying with incomplete, inconsistent, outdated, or unverifiable documents.
  • Choosing a course or provider without checking whether it supports post-study work eligibility.
  • Assuming part-time work will cover all tuition and living costs.
  • Ignoring visa conditions after arrival, including work limits, attendance, health insurance, or address updates.
  • Trusting guaranteed visa, PR, job, scholarship, or admission promises instead of official sources.
